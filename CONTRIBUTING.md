# Contributing evidence or corrections

Claim / Cause welcomes corrections, stronger sources, defense filings, court
orders, and evidence that changes an assessment in either direction.

## A useful submission includes

- the exact record ID;
- the sentence or field that may be wrong or stale;
- the proposed correction;
- a direct source URL and, when possible, a page, paragraph, timestamp, docket
  number, or other stable locator;
- an explanation of what the source proves—and what it does not prove.

Primary records are preferred. If the record concerns an active prosecution,
include the defense position or an adjudicated document where available.

## Editorial safeguards

- Do not infer ideology from identity, target, appearance, or a slogan alone.
- Do not treat an arrest, complaint, indictment, sanction, or designation as a
  conviction.
- Do not generalize an individual act into organizational command without
  evidence of direction, financing, logistics, or coordination.
- Preserve the presumption of innocence and distinguish facts from allegations.
- Update `research-log.json` and, for a correction, `corrections.json`.
- Run `pnpm verify` before proposing a change.

Automation may identify candidates and source failures. It may not silently
change an assessment.
